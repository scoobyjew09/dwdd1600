Today's News

What's in store

Today's star players include McDonald's Big Arch incident, the return of Taco Bell's Quesarito, Five Guys' BOGO deal and more! Read to find out even more new additions.

McDonald's

McDonald's CEO John Kempcxinski has recently gone under fire after a video of him trying their new item " The Big Arch " has gone viral for his robotic or rather small bite of the apparently tasty burger.

Taco Bell

After almost 3 years of being discontinued Taco Bell has decided to bring back a fan favorite in the Quesarito. It'll be back for a limited time, however if sales exceed the expected then it has the opportunity to come back to the menu as a permanent item

Five Guys/Chipotle

Five Guys and Chipotle are both offering BOGO deals for their entrees. Hurry up and get some before they run out!

Wendy's

Wendy's has announced the closures of more than 300 locations THIS YEAR ONLY. Watch out folks, your local Wendy's might be going under and you won't even notice..

Chick-Fil-A

Chick-Fil-A has decided to bring the Jalapeno Ranch Club Chicken Sandwich nationwide starting March 9th. Reviews are in and the sandwich is looking like a smashing success! Get it before it runs out.

Burger King

After multiple complaints Burger King has decided to take action and change the world famous Whopper recipe, it's received mostly positive reviews, the new recipe is expected to be in all restaurants nationwide by June.
