About Us

Our Mission

We want to notify THE WORLD about all types of fast food news, from inside drama, to new items to restaurant closures,we have it all. Our mission is to bring news to all fast food junkies alike about what's going on with our favorite fast food places, like McDonald's, Taco Bell, Subway and more.

We want this to be a place where ALL fast food aficionados can rejoice and build a community based on our shared appreciation for junk food. .

Ground Rules

  1. Respect and don't harrass others, not everyone's going to have the same opinion as you and that's alright.
  2. Nothing illegal, stick to interacting, posting and chatting with others and don't do anything illegal.
  3. NSFW content will NOT be tolerated and will result in a permanent ban.
  4. A 3 strike system is implemented, you break the rules or do something that the community deems unacceptable will result in one strike that comes with a warning. 2 Strikes will result in a 1 day ban, while the 3rd strike signifies a permanent ban from the community,
  5. Bans or strikes have the chance to be appealed, if the appeal is rejected the ban upholds, if accepted your ban or strike will be removed.
  6. That's about it, just don't be a jerk and have fun with others, don't ruin someone else's experience.
